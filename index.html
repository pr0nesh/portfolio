About Me

I'm an Associate Robotics Engineer at Goat Robotics, where I build ROS 2 software for autonomous mobile robots that move goods in warehouses. My work covers navigation, safety, docking and load handling, mission logic, and keeping robot software fast and reliable on embedded computers.

Day to day, I take features from design and simulation to testing on real robots, debug issues from field logs, and roll out verified updates across a fleet of robots.

I hold a B.E. in Robotics & Automation from Sri Ramakrishna Engineering College. I received the Bright Beginning Award at Goat Robotics' Founders' Day 2025 and won the Best Startup Award at SREC INNOVATE24.

Core Expertise

Autonomous Navigation

Nav2-based navigation for warehouse AMRs: lane and waypoint following, path controllers and on-robot tuning.

Safety & Collision Avoidance

LiDAR-based safety regions around the robot that detect obstacles and keep people, robots and loads safe.

Docking & Load Handling

Marker-based precision docking under trolleys and racks, lift and payload checks, and safe undocking.

Mission Logic

State-machine based task flows that run warehouse missions step by step, handle errors and recover safely.

Performance Optimization

Reducing CPU load on embedded robot computers without changing behaviour: profiling, DDS/QoS tuning and C++ ports.

Field Testing & Debugging

On-robot testing across a robot fleet: bag replay, log analysis, sensor and odometry diagnosis, and verified rollouts.
